About Me

Who I Am

I'm a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at Suguna College of Engineering, passionate about building modern, responsive, and scalable web applications using the MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js). I've applied these skills to build the Attendance Management System — a full-stack platform for managing students, courses, and attendance — and FoodBridge, a MERN application connecting food donors and NGOs to reduce food waste.
